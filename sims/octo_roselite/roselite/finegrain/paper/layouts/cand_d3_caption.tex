\caption{\textbf{The schedule decides the rollout.}
The two strips at the top show the same task from the same starting scene; the
only thing that differs is the schedule behind them. XPU-RT places the spoon on
the towel in 7.0\,s, acting on 60 commands that are 158\,ms old on average. The
baseline gets 18 commands, each roughly a second stale, and never finishes ---
the episode simply times out at 12.0\,s. The lower half shows where that comes
from, measured on the QRB5165 across a single 700\,ms window: spreading the
network over the three engines cuts latency from 677\,ms to 283\,ms and lets an
action go out every 144\,ms rather than every 677, so three arrive in the time
the baseline needs for one. Where two shades alternate within a lane, two
inferences are in flight at once. The halves run on different clocks --- seconds
of robot above, milliseconds of silicon below.}
