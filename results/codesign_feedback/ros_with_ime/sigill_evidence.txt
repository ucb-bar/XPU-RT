### 5. what the trap says the instruction was

`cause: 0x2` is RISC-V "Illegal instruction", and `badaddr` holds the instruction word:

    badaddr  0xe240342b
      opcode 0x2b  (custom-1)
      funct3 3     (ss -- signed x signed)
      funct7 0x71  (OPMMA, vm/mode bit set)
      vd v8, vs1 v0, vs2 v4

funct7 0x71 with funct3 3 on custom-1 is `smt.vmadot` ss, which is exactly what
`kernels/ime/ime_*_s8_ime_vmadot_4x4x8.c` emit as a `.insn`
(ModelBlaster/artifacts/ime_isa_probe/FINDINGS.md). The trap is the matrix
instruction itself, not a fault reached through it.
